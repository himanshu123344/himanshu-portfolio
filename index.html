How a project runs

The loop, in five stages.

Every Craftloop project moves through the same sequence — it's what keeps a 40-site track record consistent.

01
Research & Strategy
Understanding the business, target audience, competitors, and the site's primary goal before any design starts.
02
UI/UX Design
A clean, modern, intuitive experience shaped around the brand — usually from Figma before it ever touches WordPress.
03
Development
Responsive, functional builds in Elementor, WooCommerce or custom code — optimised for desktop, tablet and mobile.
04
Conversion Focus
Calls-to-action, contact forms and service information placed with intent — the site's job is to generate inquiries.
05
Testing & Optimization
Responsiveness, navigation and functionality checked end-to-end before launch — then the loop starts again.
